# Bitmap font provenance

`kenney-pixel-10x16.png` is a raster atlas derived without resampling from the
Kenney Pixel typeface in Kenney Fonts. Kenney publishes the asset under CC0
1.0 at https://kenney.nl/assets/kenney-fonts.

The atlas is shared presentation infrastructure. It contains no product name,
logo, endpoint, credential, or private product data.
